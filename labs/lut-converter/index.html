Fieldnotes

03 · Read me

Install

Lightroom or Classic: File → Import Profiles & Presets, choose the zip. Presets appear under your group name; profiles under Profiles → Browse.

Presets carry the look

Each preset has its profile built in, so it works on any device without installing the profile first. That's how mobile picks it up after sync.

What converts

3D .cube LUTs in the normal 0–1 range. Lightroom profiles and presets that hold an RGB table. Slider-only presets and Adobe's camera profiles have no table to extract.

Log LUTs

LUTs for S-Log, F-Log or Apple Log footage expect flat video and will look crushed on a photo. Use LUTs made for Rec.709 or sRGB. Only convert files you own or license.

HalationLooks built for stills, not borrowed from video.

Film profiles made from scratch for photos, each in four versions: clean, textured, developed, and both.
