Jarvis · Policy

PRIVACY

Jarvis is a private home automation app run by Stuart Singleton for his own home and studio. It is not offered to the public, and the only person who signs in to it is its owner.

01 · What Jarvis accesses

  • When the owner signs in with Google, Jarvis uses Google's Smart Device Management API to read the status of, and send commands to, the owner's own Nest devices, such as cameras, doorbells and thermostats.
  • Jarvis receives only the information Google provides for those devices: device names, status, events and camera streams.

02 · How it is used and stored

  • Data is used only to show and control the owner's devices in the owner's own Home Assistant dashboard.
  • Data stays on a private server owned by the owner and is reachable only over his private network.
  • Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

03 · Google user data

Jarvis's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

04 · Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time from your Google Account permissions. Revoking it stops Jarvis from receiving any further data.

05 · Contact

Questions about this policy: contact Stuart.
